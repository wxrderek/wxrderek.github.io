SSMU Research Committee

This page contains info for the Research Committee of the Duke Statistical Science Majors Union (SSMU). We run a seminar series featuring researchers doing stats-related work in academia and industry. We also run an undergrad reading group on modern theory/methods in statistics and ML.

We are actively looking for undergrads to join our committee! You need not be a Stats major (I’m not). If interested, email me at . There is a one-question application.

Reading Group

We meet biweekly on Fridays, 5-6pm in Perkins Link Group Study 1. At each meeting, someone presents on a manuscript/excerpt they read, and we discuss. If you’re an undergrad interested in theory/methods in stats/ML, feel free to show up! No registration or prerequisites required, but do email me beforehand.

If you would like to present a manuscript, please fill out this form. It contains more information about meeting procedures.
